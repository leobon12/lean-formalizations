# Gwynne–Miller Remark 1.5 reference

The docstring of the frozen realization-independence theorem cites the development copy `source/literature/gm-uniqueness/tex/uniqueness-final.tex`,
lines 360–374. That file is the TeX source of Gwynne and Miller, *Existence and uniqueness of the Liouville quantum gravity metric for γ ∈ (0,2)*,
arXiv:1905.00383v3, available at https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.00383v3 ; the cited lines are its Remark 1.5.

The remark explains why a domain metric defined from a whole-plane realization does not depend on the realization, using locality, Weyl scaling
and a bump-function comparison. The frozen theorem is the Lean form of that claim; its proof status is in the guide.
