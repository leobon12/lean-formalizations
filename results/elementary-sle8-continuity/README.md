# Elementary proof that SLE₈ is continuous

**Status.** Theorem 1.1 of the note is `PROVED` in Lean with no assumptions: the frozen theorem is sealed with two keys, and it and every advertised input depend only on Lean's standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

This Lean 4 project formalizes Theorem 1.1 of *Continuity and logarithmic regularity of the SLE₈ trace*, an unpublished, unrefereed research note (12 pp.): [PDF](https://leobon12.github.io/papers/elementary-sle8-continuity.pdf) (SHA-256 `4d8dea8b3beea33511c99ddc2f254fae1e2dc0b8d880e0a9fc27d669d4115618`). The note proves existence and continuity of the chordal SLE₈ trace from the Loewner equation and one-dimensional Brownian motion, without imaginary geometry or a discrete approximation (uniform spanning trees). Its main input is a derivative estimate of Alvisio and Lawler: [AL14] M. Alvisio and G. F. Lawler, *Note on the existence and modulus of continuity of the SLE₈ curve*, Metrika 77 (2014), 5–22. That input is proved here too.

In words: let B be a standard real Brownian motion, W_t = √8 B_t, let g_t be the chordal Loewner maps driven by W (∂_t g_t(z) = 2/(g_t(z) − W_t), g_0(z) = z), and F_t(z) = g_t⁻¹(W_t + z) the centered inverse maps, defined directly from the Loewner equation. Then, almost surely, the limit γ(t) = lim_{y ↓ 0} F_t(iy) exists locally uniformly in t ∈ [0, ∞) and defines a continuous γ : [0, ∞) → ℍ̄ with γ(0) = 0; for every t ≥ 0, the domain H_t = {z ∈ ℍ : T_z > t} of g_t is the unbounded connected component of ℍ \ γ([0, t]). So the SLE₈ Loewner chain is generated by a continuous curve.

## Exact frozen statement

The declaration head below is copied from the frozen file [`InteractingSLE/Frozen/SLE8/ContinuousTrace.lean`](../../InteractingSLE/Frozen/SLE8/ContinuousTrace.lean), which opens `MeasureTheory ProbabilityTheory Filter Set` and `scoped NNReal ENNReal Topology`. The proof body is omitted; it is `by exact InteractingSLE.SLE8.Provider.ae_exists_continuous_trace`, a provider theorem with exactly the same statement.

```lean
theorem ae_exists_continuous_trace {Ω : Type*} [MeasurableSpace Ω] {P : Measure Ω}
    {B : ℝ≥0 → Ω → ℝ} (hB : IsBrownianReal B P) :
    ∀ᵐ ω ∂P, ∃ γ : ℝ≥0 → ℂ,
      TendstoLocallyUniformly
          (fun (y : ℝ) (t : ℝ≥0) =>
            QuantumZipper.fwdMapInv (QuantumZipper.drive 8 B ω) t (y * Complex.I))
          γ (𝓝[>] 0) ∧
        Continuous γ ∧ (∀ t, γ t ∈ QuantumZipper.Hbar) ∧ γ 0 = 0 ∧
        ∀ t : ℝ≥0,
          (∃ z ∈ QuantumZipper.H \ QuantumZipper.fwdHull (QuantumZipper.drive 8 B ω) t,
              connectedComponentIn (QuantumZipper.H \ γ '' Icc 0 t) z =
                QuantumZipper.H \ QuantumZipper.fwdHull (QuantumZipper.drive 8 B ω) t) ∧
            ¬ Bornology.IsBounded
              (QuantumZipper.H \ QuantumZipper.fwdHull (QuantumZipper.drive 8 B ω) t) :=
```

The definitions in the statement come from Mathlib and from the released [`QuantumZipper`](../quantum-zipper/) library, imported unchanged:

- `IsBrownianReal B P` (Mathlib, `Mathlib.Probability.BrownianMotion.Basic`): `B : ℝ≥0 → Ω → ℝ` is a standard real Brownian motion under `P`, i.e. its finite-dimensional laws are the centered Gaussian laws with covariance min(s, t), and its paths are almost surely continuous. It forces `P` to be a probability measure and B₀ = 0 almost surely.
- `QuantumZipper.drive 8 B ω` is the driving function t ↦ √8 B_t(ω) (extended by its value at 0 to negative times, which are never used).
- `QuantumZipper.fwdHull W t` is the hull K_t: the points z ∈ ℍ whose swallowing time T_z, defined from the solutions of the centered Loewner equation f_s(z) = z − W_s + ∫₀ˢ 2/f_u(z) du, is at most t. So `H \ fwdHull W t` is H_t = {z ∈ ℍ : T_z > t}.
- `QuantumZipper.fwdMapInv W t w` is F_t(w) = g_t⁻¹(W_t + w): the unique z ∈ H_t whose centered flow f_t(z) = g_t(z) − W_t equals w, and the junk value 0 if there is no unique such z (this case does not occur for w ∈ ℍ).
- `QuantumZipper.H` and `QuantumZipper.Hbar` are the open and closed upper half-planes {Im z > 0} and {Im z ≥ 0}.

## How the statement reads the printed theorem

The statement was approved after independent statement audits (no excess hypothesis; every definition unfolded and checked against the note). Their notes, in words:

1. **Brownian motion.** The hypothesis is Mathlib's `IsBrownianReal`: the law of a standard Brownian motion with almost surely continuous paths. "B₀ = 0" and continuity of every path are not required, only almost surely; this makes the theorem slightly more general. The probability space is arbitrary.
2. **The limit.** "The limit γ(t) = lim_{y↓0} F_t(iy) exists locally uniformly for t ∈ [0, ∞)" is `TendstoLocallyUniformly` of y ↦ (t ↦ F_t(iy)) to γ as the real number y tends to 0 from above, on the time space [0, ∞) (`ℝ≥0`); on this space it is equivalent to uniform convergence on every [0, T].
3. **The curve.** γ is a continuous map `ℝ≥0 → ℂ` with values in the closed upper half-plane and γ(0) = 0 (the subspace topology gives the same continuity as for γ : [0, ∞) → ℍ̄).
4. **The domains.** "H_t is the unbounded connected component of ℍ \ γ([0, t])" is stated as: H_t is the connected component, in ℍ \ γ([0, t]), of one of its own points, and H_t is unbounded. Since γ([0, t]) is compact, ℍ \ γ([0, t]) has at most one unbounded component, so this is the printed statement. The audit proved in Lean that `H \ fwdHull W t` equals {z ∈ ℍ : T_z > t}.
5. **One event.** The conclusion holds on one event of probability one, for all t at once, as printed.

## How it was checked

The project follows Scott Armstrong's [LeanAutoformalizationSkills](https://github.com/scottnarmstrong/LeanAutoformalizationSkills) workflow.

1. **Statement.** The statement was drafted from the note, passed two independent statement audits (binders classified against the printed theorem: no excess hypothesis; every definition unfolded, including the junk values of `fwdMapInv`; the domain clause proved equivalent to the printed one), was approved under the author's delegation, and was frozen in a one-declaration file whose bytes and elaborated type are pinned by a hash manifest.
2. **Proof.** The frozen theorem has a provider theorem with exactly the frozen statement, proved from the inputs above.
3. **Two-key seal.** *Key 1:* only the frozen body changes, to `by exact <provider>`; the frozen-anchor checker, the build, an unchanged statement hash and `#print axioms` must pass. *Key 2:* an independent auditor rechecked the statement bytes, read the provider chain for hypotheses that encode proof steps (every `…Statement` hypothesis in the chain is discharged), checked the axioms and replayed the kernel check of the frozen module and of its project import closure. Its one finding (a module of the closure had not been committed with the seal) was fixed before this release, which is cut from that fixed commit.
4. **Release checks**, on the staged public tree (development commit `05c010b59`): the public source scan passed for all 12,673 files (`scripts/verify.py --scan-only`); the 59 new modules were **compiled afresh from the exported files** with the verifier's per-module settings (`lean -j1 -M8192`), one process, against the 138 released dependencies, whose development oleans Lake reported up to date for byte-identical sources: 194 seconds of wall time, at most 15.6 seconds and 3.9 GB per module, no errors and no `sorry` warnings; [`Check.lean`](Check.lean) then compiled and reported only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` for each of its 14 `#print axioms` lines, and its whole-declaration audit reached only these three axioms from all 5,914 declarations of the included modules (86,449 reachable declarations). The kernel replay of the frozen module and of its 31 project modules is the seal audit's (key 2 above); the public workflow performs the fresh rebuild of every module, `Check.lean` and the combined certificate.

## Reproduce the checks

The pinned toolchain is `leanprover/lean4:v4.34.0-rc2` and Mathlib is `a4c8ef0a69f52ec80525d5086bb3542f4660faaf`; the included `lake-manifest.json` fixes the other dependencies. From the root of `leobon12/lean-formalizations`, with elan, Lake and Python 3:

```sh
lake exe cache get
lake build InteractingSLE.Frozen.SLE8.ContinuousTrace
lake env lean results/elementary-sle8-continuity/Check.lean
python3 -I scripts/verify.py --scan-only
lake env leanchecker --fresh InteractingSLE.Frozen.SLE8.ContinuousTrace
```

`Check.lean` runs `assert_no_sorry` and `#print axioms` on each advertised theorem (every reported axiom must be one of `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`), derives the unconditional Lemma 2.1 and Proposition 4.1, checks that the frozen theorem is the provider's unconditional assembly, and audits every declaration of the included `InteractingSLE`, `MathFin`, `BrownianMotion`, `QuantumZipper` and `LQGDimension` modules (`ELEMENTARY_SLE8_AUDIT`). `scripts/verify.py --scan-only` checks the selected module closure, source hashes, import closure and forbidden proof tokens; the full verifier also compiles `Check.lean` after all modules. `leanchecker --fresh` replays the frozen module, with its whole import closure, in an independent kernel.
