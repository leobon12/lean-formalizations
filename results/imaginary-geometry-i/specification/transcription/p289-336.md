*Proof.* Write $\rho^{1,R}=r$ and $\rho^{2,R}=\rho$, so that $S=\rho^{1,R}+\rho^{2,R}>-2$ and $r>-2$. <a id="aux-98-claim-1"></a>
*The strip evolution exists at every finite strip time.* For $x>0$, the function $\coth(x/2)-2/x$ is bounded between zero and one and extends continuously at zero. Consequently the last equation in (Q2) is a Bessel gap of dimension $1+2(r+2)/\kappa>1$, with an additional bounded drift. Finite-time Girsanov comparison with that Bessel process gives a unique global law, finite coordinates on compact time intervals, a null collision set, and finite reciprocal integrals. The formulas for $V$ and $\xi$ then give continuous finite coordinates for every finite strip time.

Here the strip/chordal coordinate calculation can be checked explicitly even through the seed collisions. Set
$$Y_t=e^{-t-\xi_t},\qquad p_t=1+2\int_0^tY_s\,ds,\qquad \vartheta(t)=\int_0^tY_s^2\,ds,\qquad F_t(z)=p_t-e^{-t-z}.\tag{Q2a}$$
For finite $t$ these quantities are finite and $Y_t>0$; $\vartheta$ is a strictly increasing capacity clock. Define
$$\widehat{W}_{\vartheta(t)}=p_t-Y_t,\qquad \widehat{V}^1_{\vartheta(t)}=p_t-e^{-t-V_t},\qquad \widehat{V}^2_{\vartheta(t)}=p_t.\tag{Q2b}$$
Their gaps are $Y_t(1-e^{-X_t})$ and $Y_t$. Ito's formula, the identity $\coth(X/2)=2/(1-e^{-X})-1$, and (Q2) give exactly
$$d\widehat{W}=\sqrt{\kappa}\,d\widehat{B}+\Big(\frac{r}{\widehat{W}-\widehat{V}^1}+\frac{\rho^{2,R}}{\widehat{W}-\widehat{V}^2}\Big)\,d\vartheta,\qquad d\widehat{V}^j=\frac{2}{\widehat{V}^j-\widehat{W}}\,d\vartheta\quad(j=1,2),$$
where $\widehat{B}_{\vartheta(t)}=\int_0^tY_s\,dB_s$ is Brownian in the new clock. The initial state is $(0;0^+,1)$. On each compact strip-time interval, $Y$ is bounded above and away from zero, and $1-e^{-X}$ is comparable to $X$ near zero. The reciprocal integrals therefore remain finite, and the collision sets remain null under the time change. These are identities for the integrated driving equations, with no boundary local-time correction.

The hull coordinate is also explicit. Direct differentiation gives
$$\partial_tF_t(z)+F_t'(z)\coth\frac{z-\xi_t}{2}=\frac{2Y_t^2}{F_t(z)-\widehat{W}_{\vartheta(t)}}.\tag{Q2c}$$

Any real contact in $[1,\infty)$ would swallow the marked point $1$ and make that gap zero, so the completed finite prefix avoids this whole interval. $$X_t\ge-\sqrt{\kappa}B_t+(S-d)t.\tag{Q3}$$

*An adapted, whole-tail change of driving law.* For integers $n,m\ge 1$, start at strip time $n$ on $\{X_n>1\}$ and stop at the first subsequent $X\le 1$ or when $\int_n^te_s^2/\kappa\,ds$ reaches $m$. $$-\frac{1}{\sqrt{\kappa}}\int_n^{t\wedge T_{n,m}}e_s\,dB_s$$
